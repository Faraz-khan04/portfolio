Privacy policy

Short version: this site does not track you. No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no third-party scripts, and no forms.

What this site stores

If you use the Dark mode / Light mode button, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage under the key fk-theme. It never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

What the host may log

Like every website, this one is served by a hosting provider. That provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request, for security and reliability. I do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors. The provider's own privacy policy governs how long they keep them.

Everything is self-hosted

Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site itself. Loading a page here does not contact Google Fonts, a CDN, or any other outside service.

If you email me

If you write to khan.faraz.fk.2424@gmail.com, I receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. I use it only to reply to you. I do not add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, TryHackMe and other sites take you off this site. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you arrive there.

Your rights

If you have emailed me and want that correspondence deleted, or want to know what I hold about you, write to the address above and I will act on it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
